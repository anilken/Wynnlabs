'''Jungle Fever''' is a long level 52 quest that takes place all across the [[Wynn Province]]. 

== Preview ==
Jungle Fever has supposedly fallen upon [[Worid]], he asks the player to fetch a "cure" (64 [[Liquid Emerald|Liquified Emeralds]]). Upon reaching [[Detlas]], the [[Banker]] refuses to give any. [[Worid]] then asks the player to rob the bank of [[Almuj]] in order to "cure" him.

==Tips==
*The [[Liquified Emeralds]] given in stage 4 cannot be spent, but when giving them to [[Worid]], ensure you '''don't''' have any '''normal''' [[Liquified Emeralds]] in your inventory, as there is a glitch which may cause him to take the wrong ones, leaving you with worthless [[Liquified Emeralds]]. Don't right click when holding them in your hand, it can take your fake [[Liquified Emeralds]].
**If you do lose one of your fake [[Liquified Emeralds]], in order to finish the quest you will have to delete the rest and then use /fixquests to get them all back. You can delete them by filling your inventory with spare items (such as emeralds) and placing them in your crafting grid, or by giving them to a new class and then deleting the class.
