Quest guides (en.json, tr.json)

The quest stages, tasks and hints in these files come from the Wynncraft Wiki
(https://wynncraft.wiki.gg), one page per quest; each entry's "wiki" field links its source page.
The wiki's text is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0
(CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/).

en.json was cut into stages from the pages fetched by tools/quests/fetch.py, by the mod's own
parser (the QuestDump test), with a few pages summarised by hand in tools/quests/fixes.json.
tr.json is a Turkish translation of en.json. Where a line is the game's own dialogue or
objective, it uses the WynnChaYuan mod's Turkish translation of that line. Both files are adaptations of the wiki's text and
are shared under the same licence, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. They are not covered by Wynn Labs' LGPL-3.0
licence, and they may not be used commercially: if Wynn Labs is ever sold or bundled
commercially, these files must be removed.
