'''Colossal Ruins''' is a [[Secret Discovery]] in the [[Sky Islands]]. The discovery is about a strange ruin which used to be part of another Colossus.

{{RenderLocation|location=Kandon Ridge|x=879|y=85|z=-4674}}

*Use a spell on the rock at 879, 85, -4675.

*Enter the hole made by the rock.
