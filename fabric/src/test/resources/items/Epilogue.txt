'''Epilogue''' is an untradable pair of level 89 [[Legendary]] [[Boots]] obtainable during the annual [[Festival of the Heroes]]. It can be purchased from the [[Talespun Citadel]] [[Equipment Merchant (The Claim to Heroism)|Equipment Merchant]] in exchange for {{WynnIcon|allium}} [[Talespun Memory|Talespun Memories]], the currency awarded by the [[The Claim to Heroism|Claim to Heroism]] minigame during the festival.

Epilogue can also be purchased from the [[Talespun Merchant]] on the [[Isles of Fiction]] year-round for 7 {{WynnIcon|le}} [[Liquid Emeralds]] and 32 {{WynnIcon|eb}} [[Emerald Blocks]].
